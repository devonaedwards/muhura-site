Is your birth data safe in a kundli app?

Published 10 August 2026 by Pacefyx, Inc. We make Muhura, a jyotish app, so we have a stake in this. Everything below can be checked without taking our word for it.

It comes down to one thing: whether the app keeps your birth details on your phone, or sends them to a company. You can find out in about thirty seconds, before you install anything.

Why does birth data matter more than other app data?

You can change a password. You cannot change where and when you were born. Those three details stay true about you for life, and together they point at one person quite precisely.

What gets built on top of them is the personal part. A kundli app ends up holding who you are thinking of marrying, who your family is, what you are worried about, and what you are hoping for. Most apps know what you tapped. A kundli app knows what you are afraid of.

What does a phone number add to that?

An app that sends you an OTP has your phone number, and your birth details sit next to it in an account. That is not an accusation about any app. It is what an account is.

A wallet does the same thing again. If you top up a balance and spend it, there is a record of what you asked about and when you asked it. Many kundli apps are marketplaces that sell you time with an astrologer, so they need to know who you are in order to bill you. An app with nothing to bill you for does not need any of it.

What happens to your birth data if the company is sold?

Whatever a company collected is usually part of what a buyer gets. Apps are bought and sold like anything else, and you cannot know when you sign up who will own the app in five years.

Co-Star, the best known Western astrology app, was bought by Midjourney in 2026. That is not a knock on either of them. It is what happens to companies, and it is the clearest example of why "do you trust this company" is a weaker question than it looks.

If a company never received your data, they cannot sell it. Nobody can buy it. Not even by buying the whole company.

That is the whole argument for keeping this kind of thing on your own phone. It is not a promise about how nice a company means to be. It is about what is possible.

How do you check a kundli app's privacy yourself?

Open the app's page in the store and read the privacy section, which is what the developer told Apple or Google about their own app. It takes about thirty seconds, and it is better evidence than anything on a marketing site, including this one.

  • Find App Privacy on the App Store, or Data safety on Google Play.
  • Read Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You. Those two matter most.
  • An app that collects nothing shows one line: Data Not Collected.
  • Check whether it wants a phone number or an OTP before it will show you a kundli.
  • Try it with the internet off. An app that still draws your kundli is doing the work on your phone.

The thing the labels will not tell you

There is no label category for "this app sends your details to an outside service to have your reading written." So a privacy label can look modest whether the app does that or not.

Some astrology apps do say in their privacy policy that they send your chart or your questions to an outside company to produce the text. If that matters to you, read the policy and look for named outside companies. The internet-off test is the quickest check: an app that needs a connection to write your reading is sending something somewhere.

What does a private kundli app actually look like?

No account, no OTP, no wallet, and it still works with the internet turned off. Those four together are hard to fake, because an app cannot quietly phone home if it still works on a plane.

Common questions

Is it safe to give a kundli app your exact birth time?

It depends on whether the app keeps that time on your phone or sends it to a company. Your birth date, time and place cannot be changed later, so it is worth thirty seconds of checking before you type it in.

Do kundli apps sell your data?

Some collect a lot and some collect nothing, and the store privacy label is where you find out about any specific app. Read Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You on its store page rather than relying on what any article says, including this one.

Can I use a kundli app without a phone number?

Yes. Muhura has no account, no sign-up and no OTP. Many kundli apps ask for a number because they are marketplaces that need to bill you for a consultation, which is a different product that plenty of people want.

Does a kundli need your exact birth time?

Your lagna moves about one degree every four minutes, so your exact time matters most for the lagna and the bhavas built on it. The grahas move far more slowly, and the Moon is the one to watch, since it crosses a nakshatra in about a day. An app that works things out on your phone can use your exact time without that time going anywhere.

Which kundli apps work offline?

Muhura works with the internet turned off, because every position and every reading is worked out on your phone. Try any app with the connection off, and see whether it can still draw your kundli.
