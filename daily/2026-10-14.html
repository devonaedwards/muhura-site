Chaturthi in Shukla paksha

Shukla Chaturthi is the fourth tithi of the bright fortnight, from 36 to 48 degrees of separation, and the tradition connects it with Ganesha, the removal of obstacles, and with the honest work of clearing before building.

In the classical grouping this is a Rikta tithi, one of the fourth, ninth and fourteenth, and Rikta means empty. The old handbooks are direct about it: this is not the traditional slot for launching something you want to run undisturbed. What they put here instead is clearing, discarding, settling debts, and unblocking whatever has been stuck.

That pairing with Ganesha is not a coincidence. The deity associated with the tithi is the one asked before anything begins, and an empty tithi is exactly when there is room to ask. Monthly Vinayaka Chaturthi falls in this fortnight, and the largest festival on it opens in Bhadrapada.

The frame worth carrying is cost rather than warning. Clearing work rarely feels like progress on the day you do it, and it is the reason later work goes quickly. Choosing to spend a day that way is a decision about sequence, and this is where the tradition suggests making it.
