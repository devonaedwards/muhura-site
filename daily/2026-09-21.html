What is Shobhana yoga?

Shobhana is the fifth of the 27 nithya yogas, and its name means splendid or beautiful, from a root that describes something shining because it is well made.

The traditional associations run toward appearance and presentation in the honest sense: work meant to be seen, ceremonies, decoration, and anything where the finish is part of the substance rather than a coat of paint over it.

Yogas advance about one step a day, so they make a slow moving background against the faster tithi and karana. Shobhana comes round roughly once a month, which is often enough to be familiar and rare enough to notice.

A useful way to take it is as a reminder that how a thing looks is part of what it is. That applies to a room, a piece of writing and a conversation. What you do with the observation is your own business, and the tradition makes no promises about the result.
