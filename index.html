A jyotish tool, not a marketplace

Nobody to sell you anything

No consultations. No per-minute calls or chat. No remedies, no gemstones, no poojas, no yantras. Muhura computes your chart and reads it to you, and that is the whole product. There is nothing else to buy from anyone.

Real jyotish, really computed

Sidereal positions with Lahiri ayanamsa, calculated on your device from precise astronomy. The nine grahas, the bhavas, the nakshatras, and the varga charts. Rahu and Ketu use classical madhya positions by default, with spashta available if your tradition prefers it.

A lens, never a verdict

Doshas are shown as something to understand about a chart, not a judgement on a life. Guna milan gives you the kootas and what they weigh, without pronouncing a marriage good or bad. The reading ends with your decision, not ours.

Your birth details stay on your phone.

There is no account, no OTP, and no server of ours to hold anything. Your birth date, time and place, your saved people, your kundli and every reading built from it are created and kept on your device. We do not collect them, which means we cannot read them, search them, sell them or lose them. If you keep a device backup, that backup is yours and may include them, which the privacy policy says plainly rather than overstating the claim.

The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," because there is nothing for us to collect. A birth time is one of the most personal things a person can hand to an app, and the ordinary practice in this category is to hand it to an ad network. We built the app so that the question never arises.

Free every day. The rest, only if you want it.

Your janam kundli and its reading are free. Your daily rashifal is free, every day. Your year at a glance, the bhava your muntha runs through above your dasha seasons, is free. The panchang for where you actually are, with its tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana, is free.

When you want more, it stays simple. A guna milan reading for two people is a small one-time purchase, and there is an unlock if you want them all. Muhurta guidance, for choosing which day carries a plan well, is there when you need it. No subscription, no renewals, no wallet to top up, and nothing to cancel.
