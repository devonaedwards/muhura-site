What is AstroSage Kundli good at?

AstroSage Kundli is the app most people in India already have, and it covers nearly all of jyotish for free. Janam kundli, kundli milan, panchang, dasha periods and a large library of reading, in several Indian languages. AstroSage says the app has passed 70 million downloads.

If you want one free app that does everything, AstroSage Kundli has the longest track record of anyone here.

What is AstroTalk good at?

AstroTalk is the biggest place to reach a real astrologer. You pick a person, you see their languages, their experience and their rate, and you speak to them by the minute.

A person can hear the question behind your question, which no app can do. That is worth paying for, and a lot of people do: AstroTalk reported about 1,176 crore rupees of revenue in the 2025 financial year.

What is Astroyogi good at?

Astroyogi puts free tools and paid astrologers in one place. You can cast a kundli and read the panchang yourself, then book a person when you want a second opinion. Astroyogi says it has served more than 3 crore users.

If you want both halves in one app, Astroyogi is the tidiest version of that.

What is GaneshaSpeaks good at?

GaneshaSpeaks is one of the oldest Indian astrology names on the web, and its strength is writing. Daily, weekly and yearly horoscopes, articles, and long reports written by its own astrologers.

If you would rather read astrology than operate a chart tool, start with GaneshaSpeaks.

What is mPanchang good at?

mPanchang does one job and does it directly: the panchang. Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, choghadiya, festival dates and muhurat tables for the day and place you choose.

If all you want is today's panchang and the date of the next festival, mPanchang is the shortest route to it.

Do not take that from us. Every app's store page has a privacy section written by its own developer, and reading it takes about thirty seconds. We wrote a separate guide to checking any kundli app yourself, including the one thing those labels leave out.

Why is birth data a special case?

You can change a password. You cannot change where and when you were born. Your birth date, your exact time and your place stay true about you for life, and together they point at one person quite precisely.

A kundli app ends up holding more than the chart. It holds who you are thinking of marrying, who your family is, and what you are worried about. Most apps know what you tapped. A kundli app knows what you are afraid of.

If a company never received your data, they cannot sell it. Nobody can buy it. Not even by buying the whole company.

How do you check a kundli app's privacy yourself?

Open the app's page in the store and read the privacy section, which is what the developer told Apple or Google about their own app. It takes about thirty seconds, and it is better evidence than any marketing site, including this one.

  • Find App Privacy on the App Store, or Data safety on Google Play.
  • Read Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You. Those two matter most.
  • An app that collects nothing shows one line: Data Not Collected.
  • Check whether it wants a phone number. An app that sends you an OTP has your number by definition.
  • Try it with the internet off. An app that still draws your kundli is doing the work on your phone.

Those labels have one hole worth knowing about. There is no label category for an app that sends your details to an outside service to have your reading written, so a label can look modest either way. Read the app's privacy policy for named outside companies, and use the offline test.
